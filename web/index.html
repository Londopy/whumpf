Before you use this

WHUMPF displays avalanche bulletins published by official forecast centres. It does not produce forecasts and it cannot tell you whether a slope is safe.

Terrain data contains errors. A slope that is not highlighted is not a safe slope. Always consult the source bulletin and travel with proper training and equipment.
